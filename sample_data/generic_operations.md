# Complaints & Disputes
- Unauthorized card transaction: report within 2 business days to limit liability to $50 (Reg E).
- Card lock available 24/7. Dispute window: 60 days from statement date for EFT errors.
- Provisional credit within 10 business days after claim intake; may extend to 45 days for investigation.

# Fees & Pricing
- Domestic wire: $25 outgoing, $15 incoming.
- International wire: $45 outgoing + FX spread, $20 incoming.
- Overdraft: $34 per item, max 3 per day, fee waived if negative balance < $50 by 11:59pm ET.

# KYC Requirements
- Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, state ID).
- SSN or ITIN for U.S. persons.
- Proof of address (utility bill, lease) issued within 90 days.
- For business accounts: EIN, beneficial owner info per FinCEN CDD Rule (25%+ ownership).
